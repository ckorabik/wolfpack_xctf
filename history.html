1869–2024

The Wolfpack timeline

Scroll through the defining moments in Saint Ignatius cross country history.

Static snapshot from Google Sheets
1869

Father Arnold Damen founds Saint Ignatius College Prep.

1913

Saint Ignatius is a founding member of the Chicago Catholic League.

1948–1951

Saint Ignatius wins 4 CCL titles in a row. The school then has a dominant run in the Chicago City Championships in the 1950s, prior to joining the IHSA.

1962

Ignatius grad Tom O'Hara wins the NCAA D1 individual XC title while running for Loyola University.

1963

O'Hara becomes the first Illinois native to break the 4:00 barrier, running 3:59.4.

1964

O'Hara sets a new world record in the indoor mile, running 3:56.6 at a packed Chicago Stadium. O'Hara then represented the USA in the 1500m at the 1964 Olympics in Tokyo.

1973

Tim Smith becomes the first Wolfpack runner to earn All-State honors, clocking in 14:46 for 24th place.

1980

Mike Patton becomes the first individual state champion in school history, running 14:24 to win at historic Detweiler Park in Peoria.

1981

Saint Ignatius qualifies as a team for the first time to the IHSA state meet and places 12th, led by Dave Walker's 15:29.

1982

Saint Ignatius earns a 2nd place trophy at the IHSA state meet. A tight pack led by Dwight Gilbert had all 5 scorers between 15:12 and 15:27.

1994

Saint Ignatius wins CCL.

1998

Saint Ignatius wins CCL.

2004

Brendan Christiansen earns All-State honors, running 15:21 for 25th place.

2010

Ignatius wins CCL for the first time in 12 years. The Wolfpack also break a 28 year drought, qualifying for the IHSA 3A State Meet. The team placed 20th, highlighted by a breakout performance by sophomore Jack Keelan who ran 14:36 for 12th place.

2012

Jack Keelan runs 14:05 to win the IHSA 3A State Meet, becoming the second individual state champ for Ignatius. The time was 9th all time at Detweiler Park at the time. SICP also wins CCL this year.

2013

Saint Ignatius places 16th at the IHSA 3A State Meet, led by Chris Korabik's 15:03.

2014

Saint Ignatius places 14th at the IHSA 3A State Meet, led by Andy Weber's 15:02.

2015

Dan Santino earns All-State honors, running 14:45 for 24th.

2017

Jack Keelan becomes the 2nd SICP grad to break 4:00 in the mile, running 3:59.62. He would go on to win the 2018 PAC-12 10K title, running for the Stanford Cardinal.

2019

Saint Ignatius places 3rd at the IHSA 2A State Meet, earning its second trophy in cross country. Jacob Flynn earns All-State honors, running 15:22 for 17th place.

2022

The Wolfpack place 19th at the 3A State Meet on a muddy day at Detweiler Park, led by Matthew Conroy's 46th place 15:45.

2023

Saint Ignatius places 6th at the 2A State Meet, highlighted by Matthew Conroy's 14:40 finish, good for All-State honors and 4th overall.

2024

Saint Ignatius places 8th at the 2A State Meet. Manny Alvarez leads the team with a 15:15.

Timeline content sourced from the “Team Timeline” tab in the SICP XC History spreadsheet.
